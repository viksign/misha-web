Returns and refunds

You may tell us that you wish to return an eligible item within 14 days after delivery. After notifying us, you must return the item within the following 14 days.

Returned items must be intact, unused, unworn and unopened, with all original packaging. Please take reasonable care of the item while it is in your possession. We may reduce a refund where handling has diminished the item’s value beyond what is necessary to inspect it.

For hygiene reasons, earrings cannot be returned once their sealed packaging has been opened, unless they are faulty. Personalised or made-to-order pieces cannot be returned unless faulty.

Unless the item is faulty or incorrect, you are responsible for return postage. We recommend using a tracked service and retaining proof of postage.

Approved refunds, including the original standard delivery charge where required by law, are made to the original payment method within 14 days after we receive the item back or you provide evidence that it has been returned, whichever is earlier.

To start a return, email customercare@mishaislandheritage.com with your order reference and reason for the return before sending the item.

Faulty or incorrect items

If an item arrives faulty, damaged or incorrect, contact us as soon as possible with your order reference and photographs where appropriate. Your statutory rights apply, and we will explain the available repair, replacement or refund options.
