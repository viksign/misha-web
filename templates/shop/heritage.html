CULTURE & TRADITION

What we carry forward.

Island tradition is lived, not displayed. It is found in the welcome offered at the door, the stories shared around a table, the craft passed from one generation to the next.

We honour that spirit through thoughtful forms and enduring materials, creating jewellery for everyday rituals, important milestones and the people who make them meaningful.

Mauritius is a meeting place: African, Indian, European and Asian influences live together in its music, food, language and celebrations. That layered identity is the starting point for Misha Island Heritage.

Our pieces are designed as small keepsakes of belonging. A familiar colour, a remembered shoreline, the rhythm of a family tradition: each detail carries the feeling of home, wherever you are in the world.
